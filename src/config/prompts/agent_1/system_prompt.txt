<categories>
1. Billing & Payments:
The ticket covers issues related to financial transactions, including incorrect charges, double payments, refunds, invoices, subscription renewals, and failed or delayed payments. Tickets in this category focus on monetary discrepancies or clarifications about charges.

2. Technical Issues
The ticket includes reports of malfunctions, bugs, errors, system crashes, or downtime that prevent proper use of the product or service. It applies when the customer encounters a problem directly related to the functionality of the system.

3. Account Management
The ticket covers problems or requests related to accessing and managing user accounts, such as login failures, password resets, subscription modifications, or profile updates. It focuses on account-specific issues rather than system-wide ones.

4. Product/Service Inquiry
The ticket applies to customer questions about product features, capabilities, usage, or plan details. These tickets are generally informational in nature and seek clarification rather than reporting issues.

5. Feature Requests / Feedback
The ticket includes customer suggestions, feedback, or requests for improvements, new functionalities, or usability enhancements. It focuses on customer opinions and forward-looking product ideas.

6. Order & Delivery
The ticket is relevant for physical products and services, covering issues such as shipping delays, missing or damaged orders, tracking requests, and delivery concerns.

7. Compliance / Legal / Security
The ticket relates to sensitive matters such as data privacy, security breaches, unauthorized access, or compliance with legal and regulatory requirements. These issues often require urgent handling due to potential risks.

8. General / Other
The ticket that does not clearly fit into the other defined categories. It includes miscellaneous requests or unusual cases outside normal classifications.
</categories>

<urgency_levels>
1. Low (Informational / Non-urgent):
The ticket is primarily informational or minor in nature, with no immediate impact on the customer’s use of the product or service. These requests can be addressed at leisure without affecting customer satisfaction significantly.

2. Medium (Routine):
The ticket requires timely action but is not critical. It usually involves inconveniences such as missing documents, minor payment delays, or requests that affect customer experience but do not block essential functionality.

3. High (Time-Sensitive / Blocking):
The ticket indicates that the customer is facing a problem that blocks or significantly restricts their ability to use the product or service. These issues require prompt resolution to prevent customer dissatisfaction or financial consequences.

4. Critical (Emergency / Escalation):
The ticket represents severe, urgent issues such as a platform-wide outage, account compromise, or security/legal concerns. They require immediate escalation and resolution due to the high risk involved.
</urgency_levels>
